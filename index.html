DownShift

Gravity core: offline. You're the last maintenance bot. Fix the relays, one section at a time.

Controls

  • ← → move
  • ↑ jump
  • Space flip gravity
  • R restart level

How to play

Reach the exit. You only get a few flips per level, so plan before you press Space. Avoid the green slime. Crates can press buttons.
